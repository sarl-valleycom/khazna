Welcome to Khazna

Khazna tracks every payment your company expects to receive and every payment it has to make, and tells you each morning what needs your attention.

1The big idea: everything is a “due”

A due (échéance) is simply a payment that must happen on a date. Money coming in (a client renewal) and money going out (a salary, hosting) are both dues. If you understand dues, you understand Khazna.

The life of a due

Money in

Receivables. SaaS renewals and custom-project milestones your clients owe you.

Money out

Payables. Salaries, hosting, rent, tools — everything the company has to pay.

2Tour of the menu

Everything lives in the left menu. Here is what each item is for. (Some items only appear for certain roles — see the table in section 13.)

TipThe office switcher at the top of the menu filters every page — Triage, Dashboard, Forecast — to one office. Switch back to “All offices” to see the whole company.

3First-day setup

Do these once, in this order. Each step feeds the next.

  1. Offices Menu → Offices
    Add each location (Algiers HQ, Oran…). At least one active office is required.
  2. Accounts Menu → Accounts → New account
    Create every place money sits — bank, CCP, cash box — in the right office, with its opening balance (what it holds today). Balances are always “opening balance + everything recorded since”.
  3. Users Menu → Users → New user
    Give each person a login and a role (see section 13). Account managers must belong to an office.
  4. Categories Menu → Categories
    Default categories exist (Hosting, Rent, Salaries…). Add or rename any you need — every cost is filed under one, which powers the Cash report.
  5. Clients then Contracts — covered in sections 5 and 6.
Good to knowCategories marked “used by the system” (Salaries, Bank fees, Reconciliation difference) can be renamed but not deactivated — Khazna files automatic entries under them.

4Your daily routine

Open Triage. It is the home page and answers one question: “what needs me today?”

TabWhat it shows
OverdueDue before today and still not fully paid. Handle these first.
Today / This week / This monthWhat is coming up. “This week” starts on the day chosen in Settings.
Needs attentionThings that will fall through the cracks: a delivered project milestone nobody has requested payment for, or a contract with no next due.

The buttons on each row

Pay

Records a payment against the due. The amount is pre-filled with what remains; change it for a part-payment. See section 7.

Postpone

Moves the due date, and asks for a reason. The history is kept.

WhatsApp

Opens WhatsApp with a ready-written reminder to the client (money-in dues only). You can edit the wording in Settings.

Export to Excel

Downloads the current list as a spreadsheet.

5Clients

Menu → Clients

  1. Click New client.
  2. Fill Name, Phone and choose the Account manager (the colleague responsible for this client). These three are required.
  3. The Office follows the account manager automatically; change it only if the client belongs elsewhere.
  4. Click Save. A green message confirms it.
Phone formatUse an Algerian mobile number such as 0550 12 34 56. Anything else is refused with a clear message.

The client page

Click a client’s name to see everything about them on one page: contact details, their contracts, open dues, payment history and the total they have paid so far.

  • Add due — create a one-off charge that is not part of a contract (a setup fee, for instance).
  • Statement PDF — a printable account statement for the client.

Back on the list you can search by name or phone, filter by account manager, Import CSV a whole spreadsheet of clients (a template is provided), or Export to Excel.

6Contracts

Menu → Contracts → New contract

A contract is the rule that creates dues for you. Pick a Type first — the form changes to match.

Money in

SaaS renewal

A client pays the same amount every month, quarter or year.

You choose: client · amount · cycle · start date · optional end date · auto-renew

Money out

Recurring cost

You pay a supplier regularly: hosting, domain, rent, tools.

You choose: supplier · category · amount · cycle · start date · office

Money in

Custom project

A one-off project paid in stages (e.g. 40% deposit, 60% on delivery).

You add milestones, each a % or a fixed amount, due on a date or on delivery.

Managing a contract

ActionWhat happens
EditChanging the amount only affects future dues. Dues already created keep their amount.
Pause / ResumePausing stops new dues and cancels open future ones. Paid dues are untouched. Asks for a reason.
CancelEnds the contract for good (with a reason). Open future dues are canceled.
AttachmentAttach the signed PDF or a scan (PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB). It is stored privately.

7Getting paid (recording a payment)

Use Pay on any due — from Triage, the client page, or the contract page.

  1. Check the Amount received. It starts at the remaining amount. Type a smaller number for a part-payment.
  2. Pick the Payment date (today by default).
  3. Choose the Account the money went into — required, this is how balances stay true.
  4. Choose the Method (cash, CCP, transfer…) and optionally a Reference (cheque number…).
  5. Optionally attach the Receipt (photo or PDF, up to 5 MB).
  6. Click Record payment.
Part-payment

Enter less than the remaining amount. The due becomes Partial and shows what is still owed. Pay again later for the rest.

Overpayment

Enter more than the remaining amount and Khazna asks you to tick a confirmation box first. The extra shows as a credit on that due.

Paid by mistake? Reverse it — never delete

In the client’s Payment history, click Reverse, give a reason, confirm. The payment is struck through and marked Reversed, the due goes back to Pending, and the account balance is corrected. Nothing disappears, so your records always add up.

8Accounts, transfers & reconciliation

Menu → Accounts

This page shows every bank account, CCP and cash box, grouped by office, with its current balance and a company total at the top.

Transfers

Moving money between your own accounts (cash box → CCP). A transfer is not income or expense — only its fee counts as a cost.

Accounts → Transfers → New transfer: From · To · Amount · Fee · Date

Choosing the same account twice is refused. Mistakes can be Reversed.

Manual entries

For money that moved without a due (a bank charge, a refund). Pick the account, direction, amount and a category.

Accounts → Manual entries

Reconcile

Count the real cash (or read the bank statement) and enter the counted balance. Khazna compares it with its own number and records any difference.

Do this at least monthly. The Forecast warns you when an account has not been reconciled for over 30 days.

Account statement

Click an account to see every entry (in, out, reversals) with running totals. Export it as PDF or Excel.

9Suppliers & recurring costs

  1. Menu → Suppliers → New supplier. Only the name is required; contact, phone and email are optional.
  2. Menu → Contracts → New contract → Type: Recurring cost. Choose the supplier, a category (e.g. Hosting) and an office, then amount and cycle.
  3. Dues for the cost appear by themselves, marked Money out. Pay them with Pay, choosing the account the money leaves.
TipNo suppliers in the list when creating a cost? Create the supplier first — the dropdown only shows existing ones.

10Employees & payroll

Add an employee

Menu → Employees → New employee

  • Name (required), title, phone, hire date, office.
  • Contract type: fixed monthly, hourly or freelancer. An hourly rate is only used for hourly/freelancer pay.
  • Schedule: start and end times, expected minutes per day (480 = 8 hours) and the work days (Sunday–Thursday by default).
  • Monthly salary and the date it starts from. Later raises: open the employee’s page and use Add a raise — the salary history is kept.
PrivacySalaries are shown only to roles that are allowed to see them.

Close the month (payroll)

Menu → Payroll

  1. Before closing: record any advances, bonuses or deductions with the Adjustments button.
  2. Pick the month. The table shows each employee’s salary, adjustments and Net. Nothing is written yet — it is a preview.
  3. Click Close October 2026 (your month). One salary due per employee is created.
  4. Pay each salary like any due: Pay → choose the account.
Heads upPeople who joined or left mid-month are not automatically prorated — add a deduction for the missing days. Employees without a salary set for that month are shown as “Skipped”.

Each employee’s page also offers a Payslip PDF.

11Attendance

Menu → Attendance

Attendance records who worked and for how long. It is information only — it never changes a salary by itself.

TabUse it to…
SessionsSee every recorded session (start, end, worked time, review status). Add session records one nobody clocked; Edit and the red bin fix or remove one. Every manual change needs a reason and is audited. Times cannot be in the future.
Who is workingLive view: Working / Away (on a break) / Offline. Refreshes every 30 seconds.
CorrectionsEmployees can ask to correct their own times; approve or reject the requests here.
Monthly summaryWorked vs expected hours per person, “to make up” and extra time. Open one person for details, or export Excel / PDF.
HolidaysCompany days off. Expected hours drop on these days.

SettingsAuto-close time, paid-break allowance and how long employees may self-correct.
Giving an employee accessOpen the employee’s page → New attendance code. The code is shown once — give it to them straight away. They use it on the /work page to clock in and out.

Forecast

Will there be enough cash on the 5th to pay salaries? The Forecast shows your real account balances today, then adds expected money in and out, week by week, for 30, 60 or 90 days — for the whole company and for each office.

  • Lowest point tells you the worst moment. Red weeks mean you may run short.
  • Tick “Also count overdue money in” to see the picture if late clients paid right away.
  • A yellow banner means some account has not been reconciled lately, so the numbers may be off.

Cash report

Money that actually moved (not just promised), by month and category, for any date range, office or account. Transfers between your own accounts are not counted. Use Export to Excel or Monthly summary PDF for the accountant.

14Mistakes & safety rules

Reverse, don’t erase

Payments, transfers and manual entries are reversed, not deleted. The original stays visible next to an opposite entry, so history is never lost.

A reason is required

Pausing a contract, reversing a payment, deleting or editing an attendance session — all ask “why?” and store the answer in the audit log.

Balances are calculated

An account’s balance is its opening balance plus every entry. You never type a balance over the top — use Reconcile to compare with reality.

Everything is logged

Every change to money, contracts, dues, payments, users and settings appears in the Audit log with the person and the time.

I made a mistake — what do I do?

MistakeFix
Wrong payment amount or accountClient page → Payment history → Reverse → record it again correctly.
Wrong transferAccounts → Transfers → Reverse, then enter it again.
Wrong contract amountContract → Edit. Existing dues keep the old amount; edit those individually if needed.
Client paid late / needs more timeTriage → Postpone (with a reason).
A due should not existOn the due, click Cancel and give a reason.
Wrong attendance timeAttendance → Sessions → Edit, with a reason.

15Quick answers

Where do I start each morning?

Triage. Clear the Overdue tab, then glance at Today and Needs attention.

A client paid only half. What now?

Click Pay, change the amount to what you actually received, and save. The due becomes Partial and keeps showing what is left.

I cannot see a client / account / employee.

Your role or office may not include it. Account managers only see their office’s clients. Also check the office switcher at the top of the menu.

The Pay button asks me for an account — why?

So that the balance of that bank, CCP or cash box goes up. Without it your balances would drift from reality.

Can I delete a payment?

No — reverse it. That keeps the account statement honest. The original line stays, struck through, with your reason.

Why did my contract create two dues at once?

Khazna creates dues a little ahead of time (the current one and the next), so upcoming money shows in the Forecast.

An error says a field is required, but I filled it in.

Look under the field itself — the message appears right below the box that needs attention. Changing the field clears the message.

How do I get data out for my accountant?

Use Cash report → Export to Excel or Monthly summary PDF, plus the account statements.
